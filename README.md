# Save The Past

**Bring ancient artifacts to life with AI-powered 3D reconstruction**

---

Every artifact tells a story. Save The Past helps archaeologists, researchers, and history enthusiasts capture, preserve, and share that story like never before.

## Transform Photos Into Interactive 3D Models

Simply photograph an artifact from multiple angles, and our AI technology reconstructs it as a detailed 3D model you can rotate, zoom, and explore. No expensive scanning equipment required—just your phone.

## Unlock Hidden Knowledge

Our AI doesn't just create visuals—it analyzes your artifacts to reveal:

- **Material composition** — What is it made of?
- **Estimated age** — When was it created?
- **Cultural context** — Where does it come from and what was its purpose?

Each artifact gets a comprehensive info card that brings its history into focus.

## Built for the Field

Save The Past works where you work:

- **Works offline** — Document finds even in remote dig sites with no signal
- **Mobile-first design** — Optimized for one-handed use on your phone
- **Multi-language support** — Available in English and Hebrew
- **Private & secure** — All data stays on your device

## How It Works

1. **Capture** — Photograph your artifact from multiple angles
2. **Reconstruct** — AI generates a detailed 3D model in moments
3. **Discover** — View AI-generated analysis and historical context
4. **Preserve** — Build a personal gallery of documented finds

## Get Started

Save The Past is a free app you can install directly on your phone:

**iPhone/iPad**
Open the app in Safari → Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"

**Android**
Open in Chrome → Tap menu (⋮) → "Install app"
